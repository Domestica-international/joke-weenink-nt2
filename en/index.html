About me

Hi, I'm Joke!

I know what it feels like to live in a country where you don't speak the language. Okay, I've never experienced it myself. But I've seen it up close more than a thousand times. And every time, it moves me.

That's why I've been teaching Dutch (NT2) for over 21 years. I help people from Turkey, Morocco, Syria, Ukraine and dozens of other countries learn Dutch. Not with boring textbooks, but with real-life situations. How do you order at the supermarket? How do you call the doctor? How do you make small talk at the school gate?

My students say I'm patient. And funny. The first one is definitely true. The second one I'll leave up to you.

Frequently asked questions

Find answers to the most common questions about Dutch lessons.

NT2 stands for Nederlands als Tweede Taal (Dutch as a Second Language). These are lessons specifically designed for people who didn't grow up speaking Dutch but want (or need) to learn the language. Whether you're from Turkey, Morocco, Syria or any other country: NT2 is for you.

Yes, for the civic integration exam (inburgering) you need to speak, read and write Dutch at a minimum A2 level. NT2 lessons prepare you specifically for all parts of the exam: reading, listening, writing and speaking.

You can reach A1 level in about 80 lesson hours. For A2 (the level you need for inburgering) you'll need around 120 lesson hours. Most students pass within 40 weeks. With private lessons it often goes faster, because every minute is tailored to you.

Yes! Online lessons work via video call and are just as personal and effective as in-person. Perfect if you don't live nearby or have a busy schedule. All you need is WiFi and a cup of tea.

Absolutely! Many of my students start from zero. I'm used to working with complete beginners and use gestures, images and sometimes a bit of English at the start. Within a few weeks, you'll already be having your first conversations in Dutch.

Yes, I offer a free 15-minute introduction call. We can see if we're a good fit and discuss what you need. No obligation, just a good conversation.

Apps are fun for learning vocabulary. But they don't teach you to have a real conversation, write an email or pass the inburgering exam. Many of my students tried an app first and discovered they still didn't dare to speak. With me, you practise with real situations from day 1 and get immediate feedback.

At a language school, you're often in a large group (15-25 people) with very little personal attention. The lesson moves on, whether you understand or not. With me, it's different. In private lessons, 100% of the time is for you. In group lessons, there are maximum 6 students. I adapt my pace and explanations to what you need. That's the difference.

Making mistakes isn't bad. Making mistakes is the whole point. That's how you learn fastest. The atmosphere is safe and relaxed. Nobody laughs at you. In fact, we laugh together. About my mistakes, about your mistakes, about the weird exceptions in the Dutch language. Many students who came in terrified were chatting away after a few weeks.

Private or online lessons are ideal for busy people. We schedule the lesson at a time that suits you: evenings, weekends or during your lunch break. And because every lesson is personally tailored, you don't waste time on material you already know. Every minute counts.

At a language school, you're a number. With me, you're a person. I know your name, your background, your goal and your strengths.
