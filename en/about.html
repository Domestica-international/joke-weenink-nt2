My story

From the first lesson to the passport

Over 21 years ago I gave my first NT2 lesson. I was nervous. My students were nervous. But something beautiful happened: we understood each other. Not through perfect sentences, but through patience, humour and the will to do it together.

Since then I've guided thousands of students. From complete beginners to people who needed to pass their civic integration exam (inburgering). From Turkish mothers who wanted to understand the teacher, to Syrian engineers who wanted to get back to work. From Moroccan fathers to Ukrainian students.

Every student is different. Every lesson is different. But the goal is always the same: giving you the confidence to speak Dutch. And I'm very good at that.

Family

The apple doesn't fall far from the tree

My daughter is also a teacher. Sometimes people ask if that's a coincidence. But when you grow up in a house where every evening at the kitchen table stories are shared about students who had their first conversation at the doctor's, it's actually quite logical.

Teaching is in our blood. We both believe that a good teacher makes the difference. Not the book, not the method, but the person sitting across from you saying: "Try again. You can do it."

Successful students celebrating their achievement

Results

The best moment? When you pass.

I've seen many students pass. But it never gets old. That look in someone's eyes when they hear they've passed their civic integration exam. The tears, the smile, the disbelief.

Some students call me to tell me they've had their first job interview in Dutch. Others send a photo of their Dutch passport. Those are the moments I do this for.

And yes, sometimes I get flowers. Or cake. Or a homemade Turkish meal. That's not bad either.

The bigger picture

From civic integration to your Dutch passport

Many of my students ultimately want the Dutch passport. And I understand that. It's more than a document. It's security. It's coming home. It's the confirmation that you belong here.

The civic integration exam (inburgering) is an important step on that journey. And that's exactly where I help you.
